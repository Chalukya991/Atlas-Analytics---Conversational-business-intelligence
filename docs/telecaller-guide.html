1The one-line pitch

Atlas Analytics lets a business owner upload an Excel or CSV file and ask questions about it in plain English. It answers with correct numbers, a chart, and a short explanation, and can turn those answers into a PDF, Excel or Word report.

Say it like this: “You know how you have sales data sitting in Excel and every time you want an answer you have to wait for someone to build a report? Atlas lets you just type the question, like ‘total sales by region last quarter’, and it gives you the correct answer with a chart in about a minute. No formulas, no analyst.”

What it replaces

Waiting for the accounts team or an analyst. Building pivot tables. Hiring a data person for basic reporting.

What makes it different

The numbers are calculated by a maths engine, not guessed by the AI. Every answer shows how it was computed and warns about messy data.

Who buys it

Owners and managers of small and mid-size businesses who live in spreadsheets: distributors, retailers, clinics, agencies, manufacturers, service firms.

2How it works (explain in 30 seconds)

  1. Upload a spreadsheet. Excel or CSV, any layout. Atlas reads every sheet, understands which columns are money, dates, categories, and flags problems such as blank cells or mixed date formats.
  2. Ask a question in plain English. “Total revenue by region”, “top 5 customers”, “monthly trend of expenses”, “North vs South”, “breakdown of order status”.
  3. Get a verified answer. A summary in plain words, the key numbers, a chart, and the full table. Numbers are computed exactly from the file; the AI only writes the explanation, and every figure in that explanation is checked against the results.
  4. Share it. One click builds a PDF, Excel or Word report from the answers. Results can also be exported as CSV.
Key trust message: “The AI never invents a number. If the data is messy, Atlas tells you exactly what it skipped and why.” Use this line on every call. It is the reason clients trust it over a general chatbot.

Features to mention when relevant

  • Works with messy real-world files: “1,50,000”, “$4,100”, “12%”, blank cells, extra title rows
  • Multi-sheet Excel workbooks: each sheet becomes its own dataset
  • Follow-up questions understand the previous one (“now break that down by month”)
  • Shows “How this was computed” under every answer
  • PDF, Excel and Word reports with charts, built from selected answers
  • Every question is saved; the conversation is there when they come back
  • Projects keep different clients or departments separate
  • Light and dark mode, works in any modern browser, nothing to install

4Who to call and what to listen for

Best-fit prospects

  • Businesses with 10 to 500 staff that run on Excel exports from billing, POS, ERP or CRM systems
  • Owner or manager asks the accountant for numbers and waits days
  • Sales, distribution, retail chains, clinics and diagnostic centres, agencies, logistics, manufacturing
  • Anyone preparing monthly or quarterly review decks by hand

Buying signals (listen for these)

  • “I get the report only at month end.”
  • “Only one person knows how to make that pivot table.”
  • “I don’t trust the numbers in the deck.”
  • “We tried ChatGPT but it made up figures.”
  • “I want to see region-wise / product-wise / month-wise.”

Discovery questions (ask at least three)

  1. Where does your sales or expense data live today? Excel, Tally export, billing software?
  2. When you want a number like “sales by branch this month”, who prepares it and how long does it take?
  3. How many people in your team need these answers?
  4. Do you make monthly or quarterly review reports? How are they built today?
  5. Have you tried any AI tool for this? What went wrong?

7Do say / Don’t say

Do say

  • “Numbers are calculated, never guessed.”
  • “Ask in plain English, get a chart and an explanation.”
  • “Works on the Excel files you already have.”
  • “It tells you what it skipped in messy data.”
  • “One click to a PDF, Excel or Word report.”
  • “Let’s test it on your own file.”

Don’t say

  • “It predicts the future” or “it forecasts” (it does not, today)
  • “It connects to Tally / SAP / your database directly” (export files only, today)
  • “It’s 100% accurate on any question” (say: calculations are exact; it will ask you to rephrase if a question is unclear)
  • “It replaces your accountant”
  • Any price without checking with your manager

8Quick FAQ

What file types work?
Excel (.xlsx, .xls, .xlsm), CSV and TSV. Up to 100 MB per file.
Does the client need to install anything?
No. It runs in the browser. Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
How long does an answer take?
Usually 30 to 90 seconds, depending on the question and the AI server load. The screen shows the progress: understanding, computing, explaining.
Can several people use it?
Yes. Each person has an account; projects keep data organised.
What languages?
Questions in English today.
Can it handle big files?
Yes, up to 1 million rows per sheet in the standard setup.
What if a question cannot be answered?
Atlas says so clearly and suggests how to rephrase, for example by naming the column. It never returns a made-up figure.
Is there a free trial?
Confirm the current trial policy with your manager.
Can it run on the client’s own server?
Yes, on-premise deployment is available for clients with strict data policies.
